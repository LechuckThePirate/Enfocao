# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

First version, for WoW Forever: goals that follow themselves (level, profession, reputation, gold, items, quest, and
manual ones once / daily / weekly), the Goals and Achieved views, the "new goal" form, the "Today" window, the
announcement when a goal is reached, and the other characters of the account with BakalaoStudios DataStore.

Suggested goals: the next milestone level, leveling while rested, saving for the mount, professions behind the level
or at the top of their rank, First Aid, a reputation close to the next standing, the attunements the level allows,
a piece of gear far below the rest. Each says why; accept it as a goal, leave it for later or dismiss it. The
"Your style" form (chill / balanced / min-maxer, what you enjoy, how long you play) reorders them. Also: the
battlegrounds your level opens, the epic mount, an alt with its rested experience full, a profession no character
of the account has.

Today's plan: what to do in this session, sized to how long you play: a stretch of the goals closest to done
("Raise Skinning by 18 points"), the manual goals to tick and, with the store, the account's chores (cooldowns ready,
mail about to run out). In the new Today view and in the "Today" window; a new plan every day.

Templates: goals that go together, set in one go (ready to ride, professions up to date, battleground veteran, epic
mount, ready for Molten Core, Onyxia, Blackwing Lair). With Completao!! or Fabrikao!! installed, a goal's menu opens
a quest's chain or the recipes that use an item.
