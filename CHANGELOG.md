# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

First version, for WoW Forever: goals that follow themselves (level, profession, reputation, gold, items, quest, and
manual ones once / daily / weekly), the Goals and Achieved views, the "new goal" form, the "Today" window, the
announcement when a goal is reached, and the other characters of the account with BakalaoStudios DataStore.

Suggested goals: the next milestone level, leveling while rested, saving for the mount, professions behind the level
or at the top of their rank, First Aid, a reputation close to the next standing, the attunements the level allows,
a piece of gear far below the rest. Each says why; accept it as a goal, leave it for later or dismiss it. The
"Your style" form (chill / balanced / min-maxer, what you enjoy, how long you play) reorders them.
