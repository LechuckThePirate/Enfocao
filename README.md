# Enfocao!!

**Stop planning. Start playing.**
*Because "I'll just log in for a bit" deserves a plan.*

Your personal WoW coach for **WoW Forever**. Set your goals and Enfocao follows them for you:

- **Reach a level**, **raise a profession**, **a reputation standing**, **save gold** (yours or the whole account's),
  **gather items** (bags and bank), **complete a quest**, or **anything else** you tick yourself, once, every day or
  every week.
- Progress bars that move by themselves, from where you were when you set the goal.
- When you reach one: a line in the chat, a sound and a message in the middle of the screen, and it goes to your
  achievements with the date and how long it took.
- The **"Today" window**: your goals on screen, always at hand.

Works on its own with the character you play. With **BakalaoStudios DataStore** installed, it also knows your other
characters, so you can set and follow their goals too.

Commands: `/enfocao` (or `/enf`), `/enfocao new`, `/enfocao hud`, `/enfocao prefs`.
